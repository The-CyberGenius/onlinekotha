Exporting a WhatsApp chat is easy. Reading it? Not so much. By default, chat exports are messy .txt files stripped of all the formatting, colors, and layout that makes texting feel natural. onlinekotha.com was built to solve exactly this problem.

The Magic of Kotha

At onlinekotha, we believe your memories deserve a better home than a plain text editor. When you import your chat into Kotha, the platform instantly reconstructs the conversation. Chat bubbles, timestamps, media placeholders, and system messages are all perfectly restored in a sleek, dark-mode ready interface.

Introducing the Official App

While the website at onlinekotha.com is powerful, we wanted to give our users an even more immersive experience. That's why we launched the official Android application. The app offers:

  • Incredible Smoothness: Navigating through 50,000+ messages is butter-smooth on mobile.
  • Offline Capability: Once loaded, your memories are accessible instantly.
  • AI Insights: The app integrates our cutting-edge AI to act as a memory assistant.
