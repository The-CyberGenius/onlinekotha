When you export a WhatsApp conversation to your computer or phone, you receive a file called _chat.txt or WhatsApp Chat with [Name].txt. Opening this in Notepad, TextEdit, or Word looks like a continuous wall of monotone timestamps, brackets, and raw text.

Fortunately, you don't have to strain your eyes reading unformatted text. Here is how you can render your exported WhatsApp chat in a modern, interactive chat bubble interface online for free.

Why Raw WhatsApp Text Files Look So Ugly

WhatsApp formats exported text files for machine parsing, not human reading. A typical raw text file looks like this:

[14/02/2024, 21:04:12] Sameer: Did you reach home safely?
[14/02/2024, 21:05:01] Ananya: Just got in! Thank you for the best dinner ever ❤️
[14/02/2024, 21:05:33] Sameer: Anytime. Text me tomorrow morning when you wake up.

When you have 20,000 or 50,000 messages, navigating this wall of text in Notepad is virtually impossible. You cannot see who is on the left or right, emojis sometimes fail to render, and long audio/image attachments appear as annoying <Media omitted> placeholders.
