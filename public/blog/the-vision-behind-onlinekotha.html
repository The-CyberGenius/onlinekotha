Building a tech product is usually about solving a functional problem. But building a movement is about solving an emotional one. OnlineKotha.com is our attempt at the latter.

Fighting Two Battles

With this platform, we are fighting two distinct battles. First, we are fighting a cultural stigma. By proudly naming our platform Kotha, we are taking a word that has been tainted by societal taboo in India and washing it clean. We are reminding the world that a Kotha is a gathering place of stories, art, and words.

Second, we are fighting the impermanence of the digital age. We live in an era where thousands of heartfelt messages are deleted to save phone storage. We want to change the culture around how we treat our chat histories. They are not junk data; they are the autobiographies of our lives.

Our Promise to You

Whether you use our web platform or the official Android app, our promise remains the same. We will provide a beautifully designed, deeply respectful, and highly secure environment for your most private memories.

Welcome to OnlineKotha. Leave the taboo at the door, and step into a safe house for your stories.
