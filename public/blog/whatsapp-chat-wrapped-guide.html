WhatsApp Chat Wrapped: Turn Years of Messages into a Viral Social Story

Ever wondered who texts first every morning, who writes novels versus one-word replies, or how many late-night confessions took place at 3 AM? OnlineKotha turns your chat archives into cinematic, Instagram-ready Wrapped cards.

4 min read·October 2026

Every December, music lovers eagerly wait for their Spotify Wrapped to show what soundtracked their year. But what about the people who actually lived that year with you? Your partner, your closest childhood friend, your sibling, or your college group.

Until now, exported WhatsApp chats were nothing more than messy _chat.txt files that were awkward to read and impossible to share. OnlineKotha Wrapped flips this on its head by transforming raw conversation logs into interactive, 9:16 high-resolution story cards crafted specifically for social sharing.

What Data Points Does Your Wrapped Reveal?

OnlineKotha processes chat history in real time, calculating fascinating behavioural trends and interpersonal dynamics without storing or training on your personal secrets:

👑

Who Couldn't Wait?

Analyzes the first message sent on each day to crown the partner or friend who initiated conversations the most.

🔥

Peak Intensity Day

Calculates the single day with the highest message volume in your entire chat history (e.g. 1,600+ messages in 24 hours).

🌙

Midnight Owls

Counts conversations taking place between 12:00 AM and 4:00 AM, mapping your nocturnal gossip and deep thoughts.

✍️

The Novelist vs One-Word Responder

Picks out the longest single essay sent in the chat and counts average word lengths per message.

😂

Laughter & Emotion Quotient

Detects emoji trends (❤️, 😂, 😭, 💀) and calculates total instances of 'haha', 'lol', and 'rofl' exchanged.

📸

A Gallery of Shared Memories

Aggregates shared photos, screenshots, voice notes, and memes exchanged across years of bonding.

How to Generate Your WhatsApp Wrapped

Creating your story takes less than a minute:

  1. Export your chat: In WhatsApp, open the conversation, tap Options (⋮) > More > Export chat, and select Without Media (or With Media for photo counts).
  2. Import to OnlineKotha: Open OnlineKotha Web App and drop your exported .zip or .txt file.
  3. Instant Preloading: When you open the chat, OnlineKotha immediately calculates and caches your story. When it's ready, the Wrapped star button pops with an amber glow.
  4. Tap 'Wrapped': Watch your story transition card by card, swipe like Instagram Stories, and hit Save Image or Share to export crisp 1080×1920 PNG cards directly to WhatsApp Status, Instagram, or TikTok.
