Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about our privacy architecture and AI features.

How do I export my WhatsApp chat?

Open WhatsApp → open the chat you wish to export → tap the top-right menu (⋮) → More → Export chat → choose 'Without Media' (or with media if you want audio & photos) → Save the resulting .zip or .txt file to your device. Then upload it directly on OnlineKotha.

Is my chat history safe and private?

Absolutely. All transfers are encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Uploaded chats are stored in sandboxed user silos. We never sell your personal conversations, never advertise, and never use your chats to train public AI models. You have total autonomy to delete chats or your entire account with one click.

How does the AI capture someone's exact texting style?

OnlineKotha's context engine analyzes real chat messages to evaluate slang, punctuation habits (like double question marks, lowercase bursts), frequently used emojis, and inside jokes. When replying, it emulates that natural human cadence rather than stiff robotic syntax.

What languages and dialects are supported?

OnlineKotha supports all languages and colloquial texting blends including Hindi, Hinglish, Bengali, Marathi, Spanish, German, and French. The AI automatically detects and matches whatever texting style your contact used in past messages.

What is WhatsApp Chat Wrapped?

Similar to Spotify Wrapped, OnlineKotha aggregates your relationship statistics into 9:16 high-resolution vertical story cards (Who texted first, Late night 3 AM owls, Busiest conversation days, Laughter count). You can export and share them seamlessly on Instagram Stories or WhatsApp Status.
