Frequently Asked

Common questions.

Does OnlineKotha connect to my live WhatsApp account?
No, never. We do not ask for your phone number, password, or access to your live messages. You only upload the specific export file (.zip or .txt) that you choose to analyze.
What happens to my chat file after upload?
Your file is processed securely in temporary memory and automatically deleted from the server immediately after parsing. You can also delete your workspace history at any time.
Are my private messages used to train AI models?
Never. We have a strict zero-AI-training policy. Your messages are never used to train public models like ChatGPT or any third-party systems.
Is it free to use?
Yes! You can upload your chat, view your relationship stats, and generate your Chat Wrapped for free.
