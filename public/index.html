Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about privacy, security, and WhatsApp exports.

Does OnlineKotha connect to my live WhatsApp account?
No. We never connect to your live WhatsApp, request your phone number, or read incoming messages. You only upload the specific export file (.zip or .txt) that you choose to analyze.
What happens to my exported chat file after upload?
Your file is streamed over 256-bit TLS 1.3 and parsed in an ephemeral, single-tenant memory sandbox. Raw .zip and .txt archive files are shredded immediately from the server.
Are my private messages used to train AI models?
Never. We have an explicit Zero AI Model Training policy. Your conversations are not used to train public models (OpenAI, Anthropic, or open-source weights).
Which WhatsApp export formats and languages are supported?
We support both .zip and .txt exports from iOS and Android. Our parser recognizes 200+ locales, handling English, Hinglish, Hindi, and regional slang seamlessly.
Can I delete all my data at any time?
Yes. You have total data sovereignty. In your account settings, a single click executes an immediate cryptographic purge of your chats, metrics, and history.
