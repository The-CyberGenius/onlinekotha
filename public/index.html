Protocol

Three movements. Thirty seconds.

01 / EXPORT

Generate export in WhatsApp

Open any chat on your phone. Tap the contact name or three dots → Export Chat → select Without Media. Save the resulting .zip or .txt file.

02 / DECRYPT

Stream into browser memory

Drag the file into OnlineKotha. Our client-side parser unpacks timestamps, participants, and emojis instantly in temporary RAM. No files are written to hard disks.

03 / UNVEIL

Relive your shared history

Explore your relationship Wrapped, search through forgotten inside jokes, track year-over-year balance, and download status-ready visual keepsakes.
