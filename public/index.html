Questions, answered.

How do I export my chat history?

Open any chat → tap the 3 dots menu → More → Export chat → Choose 'Without Media' → Save the .zip file. Then upload it to Kotha.

Is my chat data safe and private?

Yes. All data travels over secure encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), your chats stay private to your account, we never sell your data, never train AI on your personal conversations, and you can delete everything anytime with one click.

How does the AI learn to talk like my contact?

Kotha's AI reads all messages in your uploaded chat to understand your contact's writing style — their language, slang, emojis, humor, and tone. It then responds in their exact style during conversations.

Is Kotha free to use?

Yes! Kotha is free with up to 5 chat imports, a beautiful chat viewer, On This Day memories, and 5 AI messages per day. A Pro plan with unlimited AI chats and unlimited chat imports is available for power users.

Which languages does Kotha support?

Kotha works with any language — Hindi, English, Hinglish, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, and more. The AI automatically matches whatever language your contact uses in chats.
